How RazorDefense Works

RazorDefense is an AI-powered system that catches failed or abandoned online payments and automatically tries to win that sale back — safely, and only with the customer's permission.

The Journey, Step by Step

1. A customer tries to check out
They add items to their cart and attempt to pay.

2. Something goes wrong
The payment fails or the customer drops off — maybe their bank timed out, their card got declined, or they just closed the window.

3. The system notices immediately
The moment a failure happens, it's logged and flagged as "revenue at risk."

4. An AI agent investigates why
It looks at what went wrong (bank delay, insufficient funds, network issue, abandoned cart, etc.) and decides the best way to recover the sale — maybe suggesting a different payment method, a small discount, or simply reminding the customer their cart is still saved.

5. A safety layer double-checks the AI's suggestion
Before anything is shown to the customer, a strict rule-based system reviews the AI's proposal and makes sure it stays within safe limits (for example, discounts can never go above a fixed cap). The AI can suggest — it can never authorize money movement on its own.

6. The customer is asked, not assumed
The customer sees a friendly message explaining what happened and what's being offered. They can accept or decline — nothing happens without their explicit "yes."

7. If they accept, payment is completed
A secure payment link is generated and the customer completes the purchase.

8. Everything is recorded
Every step — the failure, the AI's reasoning, the approval, the payment — is logged in a tamper-proof trail, so nothing can be altered after the fact.

9. Merchants see it all in real time
A live dashboard shows how much revenue was at risk, how much was recovered, and how each recovery happened — updating instantly as events occur.

The Core Idea
AI reasons, it doesn't decide. The AI diagnoses problems and proposes solutions.
Rules enforce the boundaries. A separate safety layer guarantees offers always stay within safe, pre-approved limits.
Customers stay in control. No recovery payment ever happens without explicit consent.
Everything is auditable. Every action is permanently and verifiably logged.
